# Design Philosophy

This portfolio is not a resume.
It is an immersive product experience.

The visitor should feel they are entering the digital headquarters of an Industrial AI company.

## Core Principles

- Enterprise quality
- Minimalism
- Cinematic motion
- Industrial aesthetics
- Performance first
- Accessibility first
- Storytelling before decoration
